# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AfiaCare is for students, families, and school or university demo audiences in the UAE. Users are usually on mobile first, checking quick wellness guidance between classes, home routines, family time, or study breaks. They need clear actions, trustworthy boundaries, and low-friction progress tracking.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users practice daily well-being habits through AI-supported wellness guidance, food-image reflection, movement plans, mood check-ins, family challenges, and SDG 3 habit tracking. Success means users can quickly understand what to do next, feel supported rather than judged, and connect personal routines to Good Health and Well-being.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, UAE-modern. The voice should be warm and direct, with simple Arabic phrases used as cultural signals rather than decoration. The interface should feel like a capable health companion, not a hospital dashboard or a marketing brochure.

## Anti-references

Avoid the current repeated icon-card grid as the dominant structure. Avoid generic AI gradients, purple-blue SaaS styling, overly decorative medical dashboards, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, side-stripe cards, and heavy cream or sand backgrounds. Avoid implying diagnosis, licensed therapy, or clinical nutrition certainty.

## Design Principles

1. Put the task first: every screen should make the next useful wellness action obvious.
2. Earn trust through restraint: calm color, readable type, clear disclaimers, and predictable controls.
3. Make AI transparent: distinguish live AI, fallback guidance, educational estimates, and professional-care boundaries.
4. Use culture as context: UAE heat, Ramadan, family, majlis, and Arabic phrases should support meaning, not become decoration.
5. Design for repeated use: navigation, reminders, progress, and check-ins should feel efficient after the first demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable controls, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and plain language. Support mixed English and Arabic phrases without relying on color alone for meaning. Mental-health experiences must include crisis escalation guidance and avoid diagnostic language.
